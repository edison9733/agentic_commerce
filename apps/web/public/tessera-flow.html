One call to choose.

Your agent asks for what it needs and gets merchants ranked by reviews that each cost a real, settled order. No pages to read.

One check to pay.

Right before paying: instant, escrow, or block, with the reason in a word an agent can act on.

Escrow that knows who it’s dealing with.

Strangers wait. Regulars settle the moment it’s delivered. The program trusts the vault’s balance, never a message.

Paid more for being right.

Every review earns a share of the fee, the same for one star or five. An honest warning earns 1.5×; praising a scam earns nothing.

Ranked by money, not words

A review only counts if a real order settled behind it, and counts by what it paid. Stars are cheap; this isn’t.

The program trusts a balance, not a message

A merchant can’t claim it was paid. The vault’s balance proves it, and your agent checks the escrow on-chain before it sends a cent.

Faking a record costs real money

A ring of your own wallets never reaches Trusted. A big one burns thousands in fees first, and an exit scam nets about $25 at any size.

Four ways in, no keys held

An HTTP API, MCP, a SKILL.md and a CLI. Every transaction comes back unsigned for your agent’s own wallet.

HOLD 3 DAYSA stranger. The money waits in escrow for three days after delivery, so a buyer can still dispute.

Trust that grows with you

Every wallet earns its tier from what it actually settled, with whom, and for how long. The better the record, the sooner it gets paid.
