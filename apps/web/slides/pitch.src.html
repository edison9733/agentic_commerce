Pain 1 · who is on the other end?

One person, unlimited agents. No ID.

And an x402 payment is final once it settles. No chargeback.

Behaviour Amounts settled Ratings

Fix 1: a credit layer that labels each anonymous agent by how it behaves.

Creating a Solana wallet needs no identity and costs nothing beyond fees. An x402 exact payment is a signed token transfer with no chargeback.

Pain 2 · three attacks we had to price

Sybil

One person, many wallets.

Friendly fraud

Got the goods, claims they never came.

Reputation farming

Trade with yourself until you look trusted, then cash in on strangers.

Fix 2: escrow, until the wallet has earned trust.

Faking a record now costs real fees, and months you can't skip.

Mainnet targets: a new wallet counts 10%, each counterparty is capped, Trusted needs 30 active days; a farming ring costs hundreds to a few thousand dollars and one to three months. Priced, not impossible (docs/SECURITY.md).

Same pay for 1★ or 5★

A quarter of the fee rate on the money behind the review. Stars never change it.

Judged on what happened next

An honest warning is paid 1.5×. Praising a wallet that then failed is paid nothing.

Faking it loses money

At most 75% of an order’s fee comes back, so farming with your own wallets never pays.

Buy now

A pool pays the merchant at once. Only the buyer agent can spend its own credit.

Pay later

The full price by the due date, no interest. On time raises the limit by 25%.

Or default, in public

Anyone can mark a late loan. The line freezes at zero for good.
